How We Source Our Beans

Posted on November 10, 2025

Every bag of beans at Kape Chronicles comes from small farms in Benguet and Bukidnon. We visit twice a year to build relationships with the farmers and taste the harvest firsthand. Fair pricing isn't just a label — it's how we do business.

The Art of Latte Art

Posted on November 3, 2025

Latte art isn't just for looks — it's a sign of properly steamed milk. When the microfoam is silky and the pour is steady, a simple heart or rosetta appears almost on its own. Our baristas practice for months before serving customers.

Why Cold Brew Is Different

Posted on October 27, 2025

Cold brew isn't iced coffee. We steep coarse grounds in cold water for 12 hours, which pulls out smooth, chocolatey flavors without the bitterness of hot brewing. That's why it tastes naturally sweet — no sugar needed.
