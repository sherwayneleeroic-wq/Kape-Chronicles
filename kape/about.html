Our Story

From a small cart to your favorite corner café.

How It Started

Kape Chronicles began in 2018 as a tiny coffee cart outside a university gate. Two friends, a secondhand espresso machine, and a shared love for good coffee — that was all it took. Word spread, and before long, we opened our first brick-and-mortar shop in 2020.

Today, we serve hundreds of cups a day, but we still roast every batch by hand and greet every customer by name. Because for us, coffee isn't just a drink — it's a conversation.
